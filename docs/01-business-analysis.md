# 01 — Business analysis

## Context
Latino's Barber Shop is a men's barber shop in central Brussels with a latino identity (Ecuadorian
barbers, graffiti, neon hexagon ceiling). Planity: 4.5/5 from 95 reviews; recurring praise for line-ups
("très bon contour") and atmosphere; one complaint about a junior barber → quality consistency matters.

## Business Model Canvas (salon)
- **Customer segments:** young men 16–40 in Brussels, students (student price), latino community, tourists (Sablon).
- **Value proposition:** precise fades & line-ups, designs, latino vibe, open 7/7, fair prices.
- **Channels:** Instagram (barbers' own accounts), Planity, walk-ins, word of mouth → **+ own website**.
- **Customer relationships:** barber-loyal clients (they follow "their" barber) → booking by barber is key.
- **Revenue streams:** services (main), products, tips (to barbers).
- **Key resources:** barbers, chairs, location, Instagram audience.
- **Key activities:** cutting, booking management, social media.
- **Key partners:** product suppliers, Planity (to be reduced), Robust Code.
- **Cost structure:** rent, barber commissions/salaries, products, energy.

## Value proposition of the system
| Pain | Gain |
|---|---|
| Bookings owned by a platform | Own site, own client file, Spanish/Dutch/English for tourists & community |
| End-of-month commission arguments | Pay computed from the till, per barber, tips included, printable |
| No view on profit | Dashboard: revenue, commissions, expenses, estimated result per period |
| Double bookings across 9 barbers | One calendar per barber, database-enforced no-overlap |
| No-shows | Status tracked per client; manage/cancel link for clients |
